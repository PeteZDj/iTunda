# The 100 — iTunda

> **iTunda** · itunda.org
>
> iTunda is a global farm-to-fork commodity marketplace — a forex-style trading desk connecting farmers directly with grocery stores, exporters and restaurants across 26 growing regions, with spot/limit/futures/put orders, live prices and delivery routing. Here is every one of the 100 startup tasks, answered for iTunda, with our status and next move.

**Progress snapshot:** ✅ 22 done · 🟡 52 in progress · ⬜ 26 to do (of 100).

Framework: *The 100 Tasks Startup System* by Martin Bell — Setup (1–25) · Launch (26–69) · Scale (70–100). The live, interactive checklist is at **https://itunda.org/100**.

- [x] **#1 · Identify Problems and Trends** _(W1)_ — ✅ Done
  - *Task:* Brainstorm a long list of real problems (not products) from experience, interviews and observation.
